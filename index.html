What the app does

Box, port and net volume together

Change the port and the net volume follows. Port walls, the driver, bracing and a double baffle come off the gross volume before the tuning is worked out.

Checks the datasheet first

Qts, Fs, Vas and Sd are tested against each other. A Vas typed in cubic feet as litres, or an Sd in cm², gets caught before you cut wood.

A verdict for the car

Enter your amp’s real RMS. You get one of three answers: fine, port noise likely, or over Xmax, with the box size or subsonic filter that fixes it.

Fit to space, then a cut list

Give two outside sizes and the net volume, get the third. Then a cut list in 1/16 inch or mm, with saw kerf, baffle, port walls and the driver hole.

Check my box

A slow tone sweep through your amp. Where the cone nearly stops, that is the real tuning. Then it tells you how much port to cut off.

Build card

One image with the driver, net volume, tuning, port and F3 to post on a forum, plus a project file another phone opens with every number.
